# Autoplay playtest report: Hotel Escape hot-seat

**What was tested:** the committed game (`/home/user/wt-ui`, commit `7ef0b76` "Search from the room and a
Hearthstone-style hand"), served at `http://127.0.0.1:8125/`.
**How:** `tests/autoplay.mjs`, an automatic player that plays full hot-seat matches in headless Chromium
through the real interface. It uses taps and clicks at iPad size (1180×820, landscape). Half the matches used
finger taps (`touch`) and half used a mouse.
**Size:** 110 complete matches, one browser at a time.
**Raw data:** `tests/autoplay-results.jsonl` has one line per match. Screenshots are in `tests/shots/autoplay/`.
Both are git-ignored (`tests/.gitignore`): they exist on this machine and are regenerated by re-running the
harness.

## In short

- **The rules engine held up in every match.** 110 matches, about 6,800 moves, 1,650 meetings,
  1,400 trades, 224 attacks, 35 deaths and 17 escapes. The harness found no broken rule, no console error
  and no privacy leak on the shared screen. Nobody was left holding more than 6 cards, and cards were never
  lost or duplicated. "New match" gave a clean match in all 11 of the matches that checked it.
- **One soft-lock (critical):** a guest with **no cards** who has to trade is stuck. There is no card to
  tap and no button, so the match cannot continue. It happened naturally in 1 of the 110 matches.
- **The other problems are interface problems a real player will hit:** trade cards cut off at the panel
  edge (in 43% of matches), doorway rings that are only half tappable, and doorways on the near side of a
  room hidden under the hand or off screen. The search icon can also sit on top of the Move/Open buttons.
- **A design observation, not a bug, for the owner to decide:** the hotel wins very often and very early
  (30 of 110 matches ended by round 3). See the end of this report.

## Bugs, most severe first

### 1. CRITICAL: a trade with an empty hand soft-locks the match

**What happens:** a guest with no cards walks into a room with another guest. The meeting offers only
**Trade**; Attack is greyed out because they have no weapon. The private "Give one card to …" screen then
says *"No card you are allowed to give."* It has **no button at all**, so nothing can be tapped and the
match is stuck for good. The same happens when the *other* guest is the one with no cards: the first guest
picks, the device is passed, and the second guest is stuck.

**Why it happens in real play:** hands shrink by using Bandage, Espresso, keys, Barricade and Hand Mirror, by
a Lantern burning up in a block, and by hand-limit discards. In the natural case, Victor drank his Espresso
and used his last Barricade, then walked into the Ballroom where three guests stood.

- **Natural reproduction:** match 98, `?mode=hotseat&players=6&seed=16506&timer=off`, smart bots,
  round 2, turn 7:
  `node tests/autoplay.mjs --url http://127.0.0.1:8125/ --seed 16506 --players 6 --profile smart`.
  Victor's last actions were: move hall → stairs → hall, drink Espresso, Barricade the Infirmary door, move
  hall → Ballroom, choose Marcus, Trade.
  Screenshot: `tests/shots/autoplay/m098-s16506-p6-r2t7-v-trade-pick-empty-softlock.jpg`
- **Staged reproduction:** seed 4242, 4 players. Empty guest 0's hand, put guest 1 in the East Corridor,
  walk guest 0 in and tap Trade. It also reproduces with guest 1's hand emptied instead.
  Screenshots: `tests/shots/autoplay/repro-empty-hand-trade-mover.jpg`,
  `tests/shots/autoplay/repro-empty-hand-trade-other.jpg`
- **Suspected cause:** `src/main.js:337-346` (`runTrade`) starts a private pick with
  `cards: tradeableCards(who)` even when that list is empty. `src/ui/handoff.js:134-138` then shows the
  "No card…" note and hides the only button (`el.next.hidden = true`) because no `onCancel` is passed.
  `src/game/actions.js` `resolveTrade` also needs a card from both sides.
- **Owner decision needed:** `docs/GAME_RULES.md` does not say what happens when a guest who must trade
  has no card. One option is that the meeting ends with no trade; another is that the guest receives
  without giving.

### 2. HIGH: trade cards are cut off. With a big hand the first card cannot be given at all

**What happens:** the "Tap the card you give" row does not wrap. From about 6 cards it spills past both
edges of the panel. The panel can only scroll to the right, so the card(s) spilling off the **left** edge
can never be reached.

- A possessed guest with 6 cards plus 3 Possession cards (9 tiles) sees the first card (Bandage) entirely
  outside the panel (x 38–150 against a panel at 210–970), and the second is half cut.
  Screenshot: `tests/shots/autoplay/repro-trade-pick-clipped.jpg`
- In the run this happened in **47 of 110 matches**. In 20 of them the card the bot wanted had no visible
  point to tap.
  Screenshots: `tests/shots/autoplay/m000-s7000-p4-r2t6-v-trade-pick-cards-clipped.jpg`,
  `tests/shots/autoplay/m007-s7679-p5-r2t8-ux-untappable-trade-card-barricade.jpg`
- **Repro:** seed 7000, 4 players, round 2 turn 6: Eleanor (possessed, 9 tiles) meets Victor and trades.
  Or stage any guest holding 6 ordinary cards plus Possession cards in a meeting.
- **Suspected cause:** `styles.css:752` `.offer-block .cards { justify-content: center; }` is a centred
  flex row with no `flex-wrap`, inside `.handoff-card` (`styles.css:696-698`, `overflow-y: auto`). Content
  that spills off the left of a centred row cannot be scrolled to. The other card rows already wrap
  (`#discard-cards`, `#fullhand-hand`, `.handoff-hand` at `styles.css:739, 777`).

### 3. MEDIUM (touch): half of each doorway ring is not a door

**What happens:** tapping the gold ring of a doorway anywhere on its inner half, the side facing into the
room, does **not** offer Open or Move. The game treats it as a tap on the floor and walks the guest there
instead. The once-per-match probe found this in **110 of 110 matches**.

- **Staged reproduction:** seed 4242, lobby, mouse taps along each closed door's centre line. A tap
  0.77 m from the doorway (the ring centre) offers **Open**. A tap 0.9 m away, still on the painted ring,
  makes the **guest walk there**. All four lobby doors behave the same.
  Screenshot: `tests/shots/autoplay/repro-ring-lobby.jpg`
- In the run, **20 more real ring taps in 17 matches** got no Move or Open, all but one with finger taps.
  Only 8 cm of slack remains around the ring centre on the room side, so a rounding of a pixel or two on
  far doors is enough to miss. Screenshot: `tests/shots/autoplay/m009-s7873-p4-r6t21-ux-door-tap-no-move.jpg`
- **Suspected cause:** the ring is drawn 0.77 m into the room (`src/render/roomView.js:274`,
  `inset = t + 0.62`) with an outer radius of 0.33 m (`roomView.js:196`), so it reaches 1.10 m. The tap
  zones stop at 0.85 m (`src/main.js:696` `halfAcross = t + 0.7` for open doorways; `main.js:715` and
  `main.js:727` for closed doors).

### 4. MEDIUM (UX): doorways on the near side of the room are under the hand or off screen

**What happens:** the camera looks at the room from one side, so the doorway nearest the camera sits at
the bottom edge. It is either below the screen or under the hand-fan cards. To use it a player has to
rotate the view first. In a one-door room (Linen Store, dead ends) the only way back out is often
invisible.

- **In the run:** a ring off screen **345 times in 92 matches**, and under a fan card **82 times in 55
  matches**. The harness rotated the view each time, as a player would.
- **Repro:** seed 7000, 4 players, round 1 turn 2: Eleanor in the Linen Store, whose only door (back to
  the hall) is off the bottom of the screen.
  Screenshots: `tests/shots/autoplay/m000-s7000-p4-r1t2-ux-ring-hidden-offscreen.jpg`,
  `tests/shots/autoplay/m005-s7485-p6-r1t6-ux-ring-hidden-covered-button-fan-card.jpg`
- **Suspected cause:** the low camera (`src/config.js:8-9`, `pitchDeg: 42`, `distance: 11.5`) with the
  focus on the guest, plus the bottom HUD and fan covering the lowest ~140 px. The figure also stands at
  the search furniture after a search, which pushes the far side of the room even further off.

### 5. MEDIUM: the search icon can sit on top of the Move / Open confirm buttons (and other controls)

**What happens:** the floating magnifier is placed wherever the furniture is on screen, and it is drawn
above the confirm bar. It can cover **Move · 1 AP / Open / Cancel**, the toast, and even the rotate and Map
buttons.

- **In the run:** tapping Move or Open was blocked **28 times in 17 matches**; the harness had to click the
  button directly. The search icon overlapped the toast in 5 matches and the rotate/Map buttons in 1.
- **Staged reproduction:** seed 33, 4 players. Open the lobby doors, walk into the Infirmary, rotate the
  view twice and tap a doorway. The search badge covers **Cancel**.
  Screenshot: `tests/shots/autoplay/repro-confirm-covered.jpg`.
  Also seen with the toast over the icon: `tests/shots/autoplay/m068-s13596-p6-r4t19-ux-overlap-toast-search-spot.jpg`
- **Suspected cause:** `src/ui/searchSpot.js:36` appends the icon to `#hud` after the confirm bar, so it is
  drawn on top. Its placement limits (`searchSpot.js:49`) keep clear of the top strip and the fan, but not
  of the confirm bar (`styles.css:389`, bottom + 244 px), the toast (`styles.css:401`, bottom + 196 px) or
  the right-hand buttons.

### 6. LOW: the search icon's caption can run off the right edge

The icon itself is kept 44 px from the edge, but its caption ("No actions left", "Need a Flashlight") is
wider, so it runs off screen (x 1074–1194 on a 1180 px screen). Match 34, seed 10298, 5 players, round 1
turn 5. Screenshot: `tests/shots/autoplay/m034-s10298-p5-r1t5-ux-offscreen-span-ss-caption.jpg`.
Suspected cause: `src/ui/searchSpot.js:87` clamps the icon only.

### 7. LOW (touch, timing): a card tapped just after the hand changes may not open

In 22 finger taps in 16 matches, a hand-fan card tapped **right after** the hand changed (after "Add to my
hand" on a search, after a trade, or after the full-hand prompt) did not open the card view. A second tap
worked.

- A staged test with a settled fan found no misses in 18 finger taps, so this is a timing issue: the fan
  rebuilds and deals the new card in at that moment.
- Example: match 27, seed 9619, 4 players, round 2 turn 6.
  Screenshot: `tests/shots/autoplay/m027-s9619-p4-r2t6-ux-fan-tap-no-view.jpg`
- Suspected cause: `src/ui/handFan.js:100` rebuilds every card element (`root.innerHTML = ''`) when the
  hand changes, and new cards animate in (`handFan.js:39, 55`). A tap during that swap can land on an
  element that has just been replaced, or on a card still moving.

### 8. LOW (visual): small things seen in screenshots

- In the top strip, 6-player "Room · N cards · ♥♥♥" lines wrap onto three lines for long room names, for
  example "Service Corridor · 4 cards · ♥♥♥". They are readable but crowded. See the 6-player end screens,
  such as `tests/shots/autoplay/m059-s12723-p6-r9t49-end-dawn.jpg`.
- Behind the end screen, the End turn button shows as an empty gold box. Its "Game over" label is too dark
  to read under the dimmer (same screenshot).
- Dark rooms are very dark in screenshots; this is intended, but check it on the iPad:
  `tests/shots/autoplay/m068-s13596-p6-r4t19-ux-overlap-toast-search-spot.jpg`.

## What was checked and passed (every step of every match)

- **Action points:** never negative. Every turn started at exactly 4. Move, open, search, card, room job,
  attack and escape each cost 1; Espresso was free and gave +2; a refused tap cost nothing; a jammed door
  cost nothing.
- **Health:** always 0–3. A guest was dead exactly when their health was 0. Dead guests held no cards and
  were skipped in the turn order. Attack damage was correct (Knife 1, Revolver 2).
- **Hand limit:** no guest ever ended a turn with more than 6 ordinary cards. The discard prompt never
  offered a Possession card.
- **Cards conserved:** deck + discard pile + hands + floor always added up to the starting total.
  Possession cards in play never went up, and possessed guests never became clean.
- **Privacy on the shared screen:**
  - no Possession card on the hand fan;
  - the strip's card counts leave Possession cards out;
  - pass-the-device screens never showed a role, a hand, a found card or a card choice;
  - no "possessed" wording on the HUD or in meeting panels;
  - the trade result never named a card, and the search toast never named what was found;
  - every private screen (role, turn, pick, choice, note, mirror, found) was for the guest the device had
    last been handed to;
  - the fan never showed while the device was with anyone but the active guest.
- **Meetings:** a meeting started every time a guest walked into a non-lobby room with someone they had
  not met there this round, and never otherwise. Opening a door never started one.
- **Escape and winning:** Escape worked for every clean guest carrying 3 Lanterns and never for anyone
  else. The end screens matched the state (title, who was possessed, "Round N of 8"). Dawn always came
  after round 8, and the hotel won with no clean guest alive only when that was true.
- **Figures:** standing figures were always on walkable floor in the room the rules say they are in.
- **No soft-locks** apart from bug 1, and **no console errors, page errors or failed requests** in 110
  matches.
- **Switchboard:** the public count always matched the number of living possessed guests.
- **New match:** 11 checks, all clean (round 1, everyone reset, roles dealt again, figures back in the
  lobby).

## Design observation for the owner (not a bug; no rule was changed)

The hotel wins very often, and early:
- 64 of 110 matches were won by conversion or death, 30 of them by round 3;
- 4-player tables: clean guests escaped in only 1 of 37 matches.

The likely reason is that **Lanterns are never dealt**, so in the first rounds nobody can block a
possession attempt. Every newly possessed guest keeps the Possession card, can pass it on, and possession
spreads through forced meetings. The smart bots hunt aggressively and clean bots only rarely give a Lantern
"just in case", so real players may do better. Still, it is worth a look in a proper balance simulation.
Any change is the owner's call under the rules-change process in `CLAUDE.md`.

## About the harness (`tests/autoplay.mjs`)

- **Run:** `cd tests && node autoplay.mjs --url http://127.0.0.1:8125/ --matches 100`
  - options: `--first N`, `--seed-base`, `--timing`, `--verbose`;
  - one match: `--seed S --players N --profile smart|mixed|chaos [--touch]`;
  - output: `tests/autoplay-results.jsonl` (appended) and `tests/shots/autoplay/`.
- **Plays through the interface:** door rings plus the confirm bar, the search icon, fan cards and the
  card view's buttons, meeting buttons, private picks, pass screens, the discard and full-hand prompts,
  notices, the room button (Escape / Infirmary / Switchboard), the Fire Exit Trade button, Map and
  rotation. `window.__game` is used to read state and choose moves.
- **Fallbacks:** a hook or a direct DOM click was used only when a tap failed; each one is logged. That
  happened 104 times across more than 6,800 moves and their taps, and each fallback corresponds to a finding above.
- **Speed tweaks (visual feel only; game rules untouched):**
  - walking speed 36 and camera follow 25;
  - pixel ratio 0.5 and `timer=off`;
  - the 3D draw call is skipped between screenshots, because software WebGL managed only about 2 fps on
    the shared machine. Real frames are drawn every 400 frames and for every screenshot.
- **Known harness limits:**
  - 5 "too many action points" hits (matches 10, 83, 92, 101, 107) were a harness error: random bots'
    Espresso was not counted. That is fixed in the harness now.
  - Sample screenshots of every screen type were taken only in the 4-player matches (every 6th match); the
    5- and 6-player matches have end screens and problem screenshots.
  - The turn clock was not tested (`timer=off`).
